# Signals

No actionable signal or BUY/SELL engine is enabled. Feature Engine 2.0 divergence and futures-position records are descriptive feature events only. Their confidence is unvalidated and must not be presented as predictive probability.

A later Signal Layer may consume validated features to produce named states. Each signal must record generated time, horizon, strength, confidence, supporting and contrary evidence, invalidation conditions and data quality. It must not promote a single metric to a critical alert or alter a long-term thesis automatically.
